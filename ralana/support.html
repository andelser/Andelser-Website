Quick Start Guide

Ralana connects two or more phones in the same physical space into one cohesive interactive canvas:

  • Step 1: Launch Ralana on two or more iOS or Android devices.
  • Step 2: On one device, start a room to generate a connection QR code.
  • Step 3: On the other device(s), scan the QR code to join the room.
  • Step 4: Align the devices side-by-side or as prompted and begin the experience!

Camera and device access

Ralana uses the camera to scan a room QR code. Camera frames are processed locally and are not recorded or uploaded by the app. You can manage camera permission in your phone's Settings. The current beta does not use motion-sensor data for alignment; it uses screen geometry and touch-based calibration. Android NFC handling may suppress unintended wallet or tag interactions between adjacent phones. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Beta Program & Testing

We are preparing external testing through Apple TestFlight (iOS) and Google Play closed testing (Android). The current beta is for participants in the United States aged 13 and older; ages 13–17 need a parent or guardian's permission and supervision. Apple TestFlight does not permit users under 13 in the United States. Family and friends can share experiences within these test eligibility rules. See the Terms of Use.

To ask about test access, email support@andelser.com with your device model. Invitations depend on test availability. Please do not include private information about a child or other participants. A parent or guardian should handle test-access questions for a minor. Testing platforms may collect crash, usage, and feedback information as described in the Privacy Policy.

Reporting a Bug or Issue

When reporting an issue, please let us know:

  • The phone models participating (e.g., iPhone 15, Google Pixel 8);
  • Operating system versions (iOS / Android);
  • A brief description of what happened and any error messages displayed.
