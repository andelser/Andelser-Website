Installing the beta?

iPhone
Open your Ralana tester invitation on your iPhone. Follow the link to TestFlight, then install Ralana.

Android
Open your tester opt-in link with the Google account that was invited. Join the test, then install Ralana from Google Play.

After installing, return to this page or scan the host’s QR again.
