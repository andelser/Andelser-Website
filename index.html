Philosophy

The Principles

01 / Independent

Run by a single developer. This means we answer only to our users and our own standards, building projects on a timeline that allows for actual craft.

02 / Design-Led

Good design is about cohesion. It's about how parts fit together, whether that is a physical mechanical assembly or a mobile user interface.

03 / Utility-Driven

Focused on actual value. If a project doesn't solve a real problem or make a day-to-day experience a little better, we won't build it.

Who We Are

Andelser is a registered home for the projects of an independent developer and mechanical engineer. It is essentially a one-person design and development studio. We build things because we enjoy solving problems, whether that is an ambient social app or a physical mechanical project.

Rather than aiming for rapid corporate scale or relying on bloated tracking models, we focus on design simplicity and clean user utility. We believe that great software and great hardware are born from the same core disciplines: draft planning, structural integrity, and honest execution.

We don't have all the answers, but we design our projects around the simple belief that things should be useful, transparent, and built to last. Try our work, tell us what works, and help us improve.
