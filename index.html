Sonderwave

sonderwave.app
Active App

Social media for community and conversations.

No Ads No Algorithms No Tracking

Sonderwave is built for connecting with people you already know and people you want to meet. It puts control of your feed back in your hands, giving every voice and place its time.

  • Publicly chat with friends and read interesting conversations.
  • Keep in touch with the people you care about.
  • See what's happening in your community with a place-aware chronological feed.
  • Share your thoughts or your stories or your writing.
  • See what gets featured on a special feed that moves from town to town.
  • Follow live debates or interviews.

Philosophy

The Principles

01 / Independent

Currently run by a single developer as we get off the ground, stay lean, and find out what can be built without compromosing values. We answer only to our users and our standards.

02 / Design-Led

We design technology that is minimalist and gets out of the way so users can take back their time and focus on what matters. We design across any engineering discipline.

03 / Utility-Driven

If a product brings joy or has strong moral clarity, we'll build it. If it doesn't solve a real problem or make a day-to-day experience a little better, we won't.

Who We Are

Andelser is the registered home for the projects of an independent developer and mechanical engineer. It is currently a one-person design and development lab. We're interested in what's essential and ideas that make the essential accessible.

This business may evolve or change but our core beliefs won't. We're not focused on rapid scale or growth and don't think apps or products should make money by spying on their users or selling them things they don't need. Working people deserve better.

We don't and won't have all the answers, but we want there to be more companies focused on creating useful, transparent, and well-meaning products. Try our work, tell us what you think, and help us improve.
